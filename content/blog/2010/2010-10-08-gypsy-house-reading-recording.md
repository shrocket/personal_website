On September 16, I gave a reading of my work. It was part of the Gypsy House Reading series, curated by Heather Martin, Blake Sanz, and David Daniels (all of DU's University Writing (Composition & Rhetoric) Program). The other readers that evening were the fabulous Ruth Ellen Kocher and Pedro Ponce. Here is a link to the recording of my portion of the reading (You should be able to play it directly in your browser. If not, download and enjoy!):

Thanks, Heather!
