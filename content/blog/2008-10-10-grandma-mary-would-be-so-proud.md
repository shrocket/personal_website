Today, in the class I'm TAing, one of the students used the word _polyamorous_. The professor then dared the class to write a limerick using said word. Here is my taking up of the dare:

Young Polly was certainly glamorous.  
Around her, men were oft clamorous.  
They made a large brou-ha  
That she felt in her hoo-ha  
And this made our young Polly amorous.
