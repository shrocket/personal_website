title: this road don't go there
date: 2013-06-23

I have done these drawings. I have drawn these drawings. But I have not trusted myself in the drawing of these. The drawing out of these. The poison sucked and spat. Here are the lines and here is the line. (This is the time. And this is the record of the time.) Now we will talk about the line. If one takes the train to Sindelfingen, one must change in Herrenberg. There is always walking involved. And you can't get there from here anyway. I am making a list of every zipper I own and counting their teeth. That place is kitty-corner, katty-corner, schräg gegenüber. I zig-zag through town and find myself in front of something. I find myself drawn to something. I find myself thinking, Hey, what about that?
