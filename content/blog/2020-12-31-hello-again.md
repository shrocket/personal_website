For ten-plus years, I wrote a blog called _rocket to nowhere_.^[_rocket to nowhere_ got hacked by some weird pharma thing, and blogs were dying anyway, and I thought maybe I should do something else somewhere else, and, in that atmosphere, it was easier to kill that blog than try to fix it.] Lately, I've been missing that blog. Or maybe not missing that blog, but missing having a public place to put words and ideas and photographs online that isn't "social media" or overrun with/by algorithms^[Wait, what? What isn't steeped in algorithms these days?] So this is that. A place. It is not an homage to Steely Dan.^[But it's not _not_ an homage to Steely Dan (either the band or the dildo).] It will not have a theme.^[Other than whatever themes it eventually has, which remain to be seen.] It will not attempt to monetize itself or anything else. It will not be concerned with SEO or number of visitors.^[Though its author might, upon occasion, wonder how many people are reading along.] It will not venture much further than this into the realm of manifesto.^[Please see pages 66-67 of the October 2015 issue of the fantastic literary journal _\]trash\[pool_.]
