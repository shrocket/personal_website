Have you heard the one about why you can't ask ethnic minority _X_ the time in the morning? Because they will spill coffee in their lap. Then a gesture wherein the right hand is held as though it is holding a cup of coffee and turned 90 degrees toward the body as though to look at a watch. Then the obvious question from a member of the audience: But who wears watches anymore? A valid question despite the fact that this small town boasts of/sustains at least one shop dedicated entirely to timepieces and also several jewelers. The German word for jewelry is _Schmuck_. Big signs that announce _SCHMUCK_! These days, only schmucks wear watches. The hip check their phones, any one of the several public clocks, or simply listen for the bells. Ethnic minority _X_ is, of course, the same minority whose navy installed screen doors on its submarines. The lap drowns in hot liquid, the sailor in cold. In a sense, the semen is scalded, the seaman crushed. One wonders how ethnic minority _X_ continues to reproduce at such a prodigious pace. Or even understand the jokes told about them; they never learn the language.

You have, through an unconscious mental algebra, solved for _X_, but your solution is not my solution. Perhaps you are better at math than I am. I always get stuck trying to balance the equation. I don't even know what that means. Please turn to the back of this blog for the solution. There, you will also find the answer to the question _What is this blog about?_, as well as a monster. That monster is Grover. Grover is the monster at the end of this blog. Ask _him_ the time.
