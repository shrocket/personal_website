The seed was black. She sucked a seed from between two of her teeth; the seed was black. After swallowing the last of the mashed potatoes, she sucked a seed from between two of her teeth; the seed was black. She set her glass of white wine down on the bleached linen tablecloth, ate and swallowed the last of the mashed potatoes, and then sucked a seed from between two of her teeth; the seed was black. She complimented her hostess, Betty White, on the flakiness of the trout in bechamel sauce, set her glass of white wine of the bleached linen tablecloth, swallowed the last of the mashed potatoes, and then sucked a seed from between two of her teeth; the seed was black. The tones of the piano piece by Erik Satie died down, so she complimented her hostess, Betty White, again on the flakiness of the trout in bechamel sauce, set her glass of white wine back down on the bleached linen tablecloth, ate the last of the mashed potatoes, and sucked a seed from between two of her teeth; the seed was black.
