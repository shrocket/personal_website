And honestly, it's really hard to know if each moment is its own unique, discrete thing, or if it's the same as every other moment because, you know, if you stack everything on top of everything and look down on it, even if everything is clear, the stacking causes distortions and blendings, and those supposedly discrete, unique moments get subsumed. But where was I? That's right: nowhere and everywhere. I guess I could complain about the energy required to pinpoint myself into a point of view, but it doesn't require any energy at all. Or it requires all the energy, but there I go again. If, in order to explain anything, I have to shrink myself down into a single anything, I most certainly, from that nothing but limited perspective, cannot even begin to explain limitlessness. The true voice of God is the sound emitted by every single subatomic particle as well as the sound emitted by the infinite space between each and all of those particles. I feel like I've done this before.
