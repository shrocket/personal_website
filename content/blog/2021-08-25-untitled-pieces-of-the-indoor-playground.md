My entire, extended family and many friends lived in a labyrinthine, crumbling mansion. Everyone was either sick or malnourished. My cigarette case was broken. I wanted to show someone why adding a water feature to the grounds made no economic sense. Pieces of the indoor playground came off in my hands. Someone gave me several dice to hide for the wedding reception. My sister had the biggest room.
