1. Here is something from my work notebook:  
   "ein bisschen fickig"  
   which was used to mean "a little fiddly" but in a quite negative way (the literal translation would be "a little fucky").

2. My colleague asked me to look at the cyber security quiz she was taking. The question was about the definition of "smishing." One of the answers was something like "the new single from Smashing Pumpkins." My colleague thought that was very funny. I said it was a little odd because they hadn't released a single in years, and my colleague was SHOCKED to find out they were a real band.

3. Driving through Bad-Bergzabern, I saw the following inside of 30 seconds:  
   a. a man in a mobility scooter having trouble navigating a curb  
   b. 30 boys in red shirts jogging through town  
   c. a small group of people, 2/3 of whom were blind, cross the street
