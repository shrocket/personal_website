A couple of weeks ago, while trying to find books about the Mass Observation movement in Britain in the first half of the 20th century, I found this website ([WorldCat.org](http://www.worldcat.org/)) that connects the collections of hundreds of libraries all over the world. It's great fun to search for a book and find out the closest one (of which the website is aware) is thousands of miles away.

I've also started using WorldCat and del.icio.us together to create a [list of what I've been reading](http://del.icio.us/shrocket/recentlyread). I figured the sidebar had links to what I've been photographing, listening to, and looking at on the internet, it should probably also show what I've been reading. It's a short list because I just started it, and decided to only go back as far as my recent move. I haven't read much since moving.
