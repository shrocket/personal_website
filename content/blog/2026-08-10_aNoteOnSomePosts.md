Because this blog contains 20+ years of blogging, you may, in browsing, come across post, an opinion, a bit of language that feels wrong, that is wrong.

When I converted my two previous blogs to markdown so that I could post them to this blog, I read through everything and found some things I didn't like. I thought long and hard about whether to omit them from the archive. Instead, I added disclaimers. Disclaimers do not erase badness. They attempt to say, "I no longer claim this language or these thoughts," but they can't really do that, can they? I thought those thoughts. I wrote those words. I posted them to the internet for everyone to see. The best a disclaimer can do now, I think, is this: I have seen this; I know it is wrong; I am trying not to be this person any more.
