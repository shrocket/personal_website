I'm being followed on [Twitter](http://twitter.com/#!/shrocket) by at least one bot that thinks it's . . . going to college? participating in a lot (and I mean **a lot**) of sports? having a great time? not feeling well? I don't honestly know. But whatever this is, every single one of its tweets is constructed just like the title of this post. And that's all it seems to do. It doesn't appear, at the moment, to be linking to enlargement pills or porn. And there seem to be multiple versions of this bot. The one that just "decided" to follow me is "named" Jnie Carillo, but I found others by googling one of its tweets. There's one called Tilda Cohan, one called Brianne Messner, and even one called Alfredia Buchholz.
