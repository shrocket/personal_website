The OED defines the word _apposition_ these ways: 1. The action of putting or placing one thing to another; application. †2. That which is put to or added; an addition. 3. The placing of things in close superficial contact; the putting of distinct things side by side in close proximity. 4. The fact or condition of being in close contact, juxtaposition, parallelism.

It is different from _juxtaposition_ in that it is more (as you can see above, _apposition_ contains _juxtaposition_).

Harper's Weekly (an email news review to which you can subscribe by visiting [this link](http://harpers.org) or [this link](mailto:join-harpers-weekly@pluto.sparklist.com)) is particularly adept at apposition. Here is a good example:

> Jack "Dr. Death" Kevorkian, who participated in more than 130 assisted suicides before a 2000 murder conviction, died of natural causes at 83, and Queenie, the world's only waterskiing elephant, was euthanized at 59.
