I went to a lecture last week by a visiting professor from the U.S. The audience was mostly made up of German undergrads. During the lecture (which was really quite interesting), I made a list of words used by the professor with which German undergrads would likely have some trouble. I am not judging the professor for using them. I just found myself keeping track, and thought the resulting list was interesting enough to share. To wit:

> askance, accommodate, aggregate, appraisal, apt, augmented, auspices, blighted, crowd-sourcing, decennial, deficit, deviant, ecumenical, embargo, enumeration, fraternal, fundamentally, householder, hybrid, hyper-abundance, iconoclasm, inductively, inextricably, infiltration, inherently, insularity, interlocutor, interstices, intertwined, monetized, numerator, occluded, ossified, parcel, proprietary, proprietress, pulp, repository, scarcity, sea-change, spatialization, stevedores, subversive, trans-domain, triangulate, ubiquity, urbanization, vagrancy, vector, vernacular, vice-segregation, whiz-bang
