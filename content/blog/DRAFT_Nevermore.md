_Nevermore_ was written by Marie Redonnet and translated by Jordan Stump. The edition I read was published in 1996 by the University of Nebraska Press.

I read this book for the first time in probably 2008. Over the last week or so, I read it twice in a row.

Here is a complete list of characters from the book (in order of appearance):

1. Willy Bost (deputy in San Rosa)
2. Commander Roney Burke (commander in San Rosa)
3. Cassy Mac Key (singer at the Babylon)
4. Dany Sapin (publisher of the _Gazette_)
5. President Hardley (president of Fuller Bank and governor of San Rosa)
6. President Patter (president of the council)
7. Rose Dore (owner of the Eden Palace)
8. Drove Wrangler (manager of the Grand Garage)
9. Lizzie Malik (owner of the Gold House and former acrobat in the Fuch Circus)
10. Dora Atter (owner of the Babylon and the yacht _Salve Regina_)
11. Gobbs (local tycoon and owner of the yacht _Moby Dick_)
12. Tony Landry (film director)
13. Mattie (former costumer for the Fuch Circus)
14. Livio (former magician for the Fuch Circus and adopted son of Nina and Nino)
15. Lizzie's grandmother
16. Sister Cize (nun at the Holy Savior rest home)
17. Father Anders (priest at Santa Cruce church)
18. Nino (dwarf of the Fuch Circus and Nina's husband)
19. Nina (giantess of the Fuch Circus and Nino's wife)
20. Cidie (Dora Atter's companion)
21. Goppy (hunchback of the Fuch Circus)
22. Gina Koll (cashier at the Grand Garage)
23. Stive Lenz (cameraman)

In 2003, before I had any knowledge of Marie Redonnet or _Nevermore_, I was in the process of writing an office memo every day, a project I worked on for a year. One day, late that year, a friend of mine sent me a list compiled by Paul Auster of the common names for various North American plants. I made a _memo_ with it. Here is a reproduction of that memo:

<h2><center>memo</center></h2>

To: C  
From: &W  
Re: _Another Notch on My Belt_, a play in five acts (with special thanks to Paul Auster via ECS)

## Cast of Characters

**Dyer's Woad**: The Sheriff—a man without one single morally confused bone in his body  
**Curlycup Gunweed**: The Outlaw, and itchy-trigger-fingered, all-around bad man  
**Labriform Milkweed**: The most beautiful woman in the whole of the New Mexico Territory  
**Purple Deadnettle**: Gunweed’s sidekick, known for his knifework  
**Silky Crazyweed**: Gunweed’s spurned, estranged, vengeful, hateful, spiteful wife  
**Riddell Groundsel**: The crazy old coot who speaks the confusing, oracular truth

### The Cowboys/Gunslingers:

**Bur Chervil**: The Sheriff’s best friend  
**Bristly Hawksbeard**: The muscle  
**Spineless Horsebrushy**: The coward  
**Mexican Sprangletop**: The strong, silent type from south of the border

### The Showgirls:

**Plumeless Thistle**: The madame
**Squarerose Knapweed**: The ugly one
**Tansy Ragwort**: The heart of gold  
**Velvety Gaura**: The naughty one

### The Townsfolk:

**Hairy Fleasbane**: The saloon owner  
**Smallseed Falseflax**: The general store owner  
**Flixwood Tansymustard**: The minister  
**Clasping Pepperweed**: The spinster

### The Down-on-their-luck Golddiggers (non-speaking, should be used as set-dressing):

Skeletonleaf Bursage, Common Sagewort, Nodding Beggarsticks, Poverty Sumpweed, Ripgut Brome

### The Talking Horses:

Rattail Fescue, Dalmatian Toadflax, Bilobed Speedwell
