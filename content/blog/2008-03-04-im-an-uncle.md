\[updated at 2:29 p.m.\]

My sister gave birth to an as-of-yet-unnamed baby **girl** this morning at **10:07**. She weighed in at **7lb. 8oz.**, and is **19.75"** long! Baby, mother, and father are all doing well (despite the fact that both mom and dad have influenza B)!

Just as the baby isn't yet sure what she'll be called, J and I are unsure of what we'll be called. We're currently mulling over the idea of simply having the child refer to us as Doctor.

Hooray!

That's a joke about "Doctor." Probably we'll let her call us whatever she wants, even if it's "Late For Dinner."
